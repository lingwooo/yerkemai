Designed for flexible practice

How the features can support learning

Different learners need different combinations of support. YerkemAi brings adjustable presentation, audio, short practice steps and clear feedback together so each learner can choose what works for them.

01 · READ

Adjust the reading view

Use larger text, more spacing, a calm page tone, high contrast or reduced motion.

Can make text presentation easier to tailor and reduce visual distractions.
02 · LISTEN

Pair text with audio

Listen to a sentence or vocabulary word while following the written text.

Offers another way to access the same word or instruction.
03 · PRACTISE

Work in short steps

Read a few lines, turn over a vocabulary card, or answer one question at a time.

Keeps each activity focused and lets the learner set the pace.
04 · REVIEW

See a useful next step

Aiko explains selected beginner English patterns and gives one small practice prompt.

Makes feedback specific and gives the learner a clear action to try.

Designed for choice, not a one-size-fits-all fix. Some readers prefer a different font, spacing or background from others. Try the settings with the learner and keep the ones they find useful.

What to notice

Look for small signs that a tool is helping.

These are practical learning observations, not clinical measures or promised outcomes. Use them to decide together which settings and activities to keep.

Getting startedCan the learner begin an activity with fewer prompts?
Keeping their placeCan they follow the current line or step comfortably?
Remembering wordsCan they recall a practiced word after a short break?
Choosing supportDo they use audio, spacing or a page theme when it helps?
